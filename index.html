How the numbers are computed

Lesson speed (WPM) = (accepted keystrokes ÷ 5) ÷ elapsed minutes, measured from first keystroke to lesson completion, excluding explicit pauses. Errors are included in the count, as on keybr.com. Restricted-key lessons measure what you practiced, not a full-keyboard exam speed.

Accuracy = accepted ÷ (accepted + errors) per lesson. Overall figures average per-lesson values.

Per-key speed = 12,000 ÷ mean clean milliseconds per hit on that key (equivalent WPM if every key were that fast). The top and bottom 10% of timings in a lesson are trimmed before averaging, so one stumble or one lucky burst does not move the number.

Score = CPM × distinct keys used ÷ (errors + 1) × (accepted ÷ 50). It rewards fast, varied, clean lessons.

Smoothing on the speed chart is exponential: smoothed = α·value + (1−α)·previous, with α = 10−3×smoothness (the same formula keybr.com uses). The dashed trend line is a least-squares fit over the smoothed values.

Calendar shading uses your actual stored practice seconds per day against your daily goal setting.

How measurements work

Practice WPM = accepted Remington input units ÷ 5 ÷ minutes, including spaces. An Alt-code glyph is one input unit; modifier/numpad digits are not separate text units. Hindi words/minute counts completed space-separated words. Accuracy counts every wrong attempt equally, including matras and conjuncts. No guessed exam marks. Per-key results use clean intervals, grouped per finished lesson and smoothed at 0.1. Pauses remain in lesson speed but intervals over 12 seconds are excluded from key calibration.
